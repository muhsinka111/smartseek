House rules

eight lines · no fine print

One rule does everything:

A position's rank is the total dollars behind it. Everything below is just how that rule stays honest.

  1. 01Money is the only ranking. No algorithm, no jury, no engagement metrics.
  2. 02New positions start at $1. Whole dollars only — we keep arithmetic honest.
  3. 03Taking № 1 costs the leader plus $1. Lower bids still buy whatever rank they can reach.
  4. 04Raise anytime, pay the difference. Your past belief stays banked on the ledger.
  5. 05Ties go to the earlier believer. Conviction that arrives first keeps the higher seat.
  6. 06Bids are final. Getting outbid isn't a loss — it's the market working.
  7. 07Clicks pass straight through, counted honestly and printed beside your name.
  8. 08Everything is published. Bids, history, totals — the ledger belongs to everyone.

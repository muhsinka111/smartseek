A board you can read

SmartSeek stays simple on purpose. These rules keep ranking auditable, payment honest, and every listing useful to the person clicking it.

1 / Ranking is total paid

The highest confirmed total is #1, the next highest is #2, and so on. There is no hidden quality score or editorial override.

2 / Start low, move one dollar at a time

New listings start at $1. To move above a listing, pay at least $1 more than its public total. The maximum public total is $100,000.

3 / Categories are independent leagues

The global board shows the full market. Category tabs show who leads inside a specific lane, so a specialist can win relevance without needing to beat every kind of product at once.

4 / Payment confirms the move

Checkout is processed through Stripe. A pending Checkout is not public and cannot change rank. Only a completed payment enters the ledger; confirmed bids are non-refundable.

5 / The link is the product

Every public listing points to its destination through a tracked redirect. Clicks are counted before the visitor leaves SmartSeek. No fake traffic is created to make the board look busy.

6 / Keep the board useful

Illegal, fraudulent, abusive, misleading, or infringing listings may be removed without refund. SmartSeek is a discovery channel, not a guarantee of impressions, clicks, or sales.

Rules

Rank is determined by total paid. Highest bid wins. Minimum bid is $1. Bids are public.

Revenue

SmartSeek collects a small platform fee on paid bids. The rest stays with the listing owner.

Rules

These are the current rules of the board. They can change, but never retroactively.

Ranking

Rank is determined by total paid. The highest paid position is #1, second highest is #2, and so on.

Minimum bid

New listings start at $1. To move up, bid at least $1 more than the listing directly above you.

Taking #1

The top spot is always takeable. If you want #1, bid enough to beat the current leader.

Ties

Equal bids keep their existing order. The older paid position remains higher.

Payment

Payments are processed through Stripe. Completed payments confirm rank. Bids are non-refundable once confirmed.

Visibility

All bids are public. Clicks are counted from the board link to the listed domain.

Prohibited content

Illegal, fraudulent, or abusive listings may be removed without refund.

About SmartSeek

SmartSeek is a transparent pay-to-rank discovery board. The highest bid wins. No algorithm, no dashboard, no waiting — just a live list where rank is exactly what someone paid.

Why it exists

Most discovery is decided by opaque systems. SmartSeek makes the market explicit: buyers see what’s smart, sellers compete with money instead of guesswork, and every price is public.

Who it’s for

AI tools, SaaS, data products, platforms, and anything “smart.” If your audience searches for the best option, you can be seen first.

What it costs

New listings start at $1. To move up, bid $1 more than the listing above you. The #1 spot is always takeable.

Contact

Questions, press, or partnerships: info@smartseek.com

Rules

Rank is determined by total paid. Highest bid wins. Minimum bid is $1. Bids are public.

Revenue

SmartSeek collects a small platform fee on paid bids. The rest stays with the listing owner.
